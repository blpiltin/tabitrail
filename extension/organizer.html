Tabs open instantly with their saved title, but the page itself only loads when you switch to that tab. This keeps hundreds of restored tabs from freezing your browser or computer.

Categories named like "Home: Finances" and "Home: Kids" share one window called "Home", with each category as a tab group inside it. Categories without a prefix go to a window called "Other" (or, if most of your categories have no prefix, to the window that held most of their links). Uncheck "Keep each category in one window" to open each link in the exact window it was saved from, or uncheck "Restore tabs into separate windows" to open everything in the current window.
